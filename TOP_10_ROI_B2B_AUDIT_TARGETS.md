# Top 10 Highest ROI B2B Security Audit Targets

## Overview

## ROI Calculation Methodology
- **Time Savings**: Reduction in bypass time vs. traditional methods
- **Cost Efficiency**: Hardware investment vs. security improvement
- **Scalability**: Ability to audit multiple vehicles/fleets
- **Commercial Value**: Market demand and pricing potential
